How to interpret this terminal
EDUCATIONAL USE ONLY

Synthetic pricesAll prices, volumes, ticks and book levels are generated locally for demonstration. They are not exchange quotes, historical market observations, or investment recommendations.

Matching assumptionsMarket orders sweep the displayed opposite-side levels in price order. Limit orders rest in the book unless they cross the opposite best quote, in which case the crossing portion executes first.

Production boundaryA live multi-user terminal needs a trusted server-side matching engine, persistent storage, authenticated sessions, validated market-data adapters, monitoring, and security controls.
